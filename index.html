FAQ

Quick answers.

What is PrinTribe?

PrinTribe is made-to-order framing for people who sell art online. Connect your store and we print, frame, and ship each order under your name, with no stock to hold and no subscription to pay.

How am I billed?

Per order, charged when your shipping label prints. No monthly fee and no per-variant cost, just production plus flat-rate shipping.

What can you make, and in what sizes?

Framed and unframed paper prints, framed canvas, and gallery-wrap canvas, in any size from 4×4 up to 32×40 and in fractional increments.

What if an order arrives damaged?

We remake and reship it for free within two business days. No photo and no claim form. It is on us, not you.

Who owns the art I upload?

You do, always. Uploading only lets us produce and ship your orders, and that permission ends the moment you leave. We never train models on your work or share it.

You make the art. We will make the frames.
